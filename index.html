Methodology & limitations

Built-up expansion is estimated by comparing the supplied 2013 and 2023 datasets. Results depend on the source geometry, projection, classification, topology and area attribute quality.

When polygons represent the same area at both dates, do not add their areas together as if they were independent. The net difference is not automatically the newly developed land area.

This is a screening and exploratory analysis. It does not independently validate satellite classifications or prove compliance with SDG 11.
